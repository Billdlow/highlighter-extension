# Privacy Policy — Highlighter Notes

**Last updated: September 2026**

Highlighter Notes is a personal, independent browser extension. This page explains what data it handles and where that data goes.

## What data the extension collects

When you highlight text on a web page, the extension saves the following to your browser's local storage (`chrome.storage.local`), on your own device:

- The highlighted text, its color, and any note you write
- The source page's URL, title, and the time of the highlight
- The topic/category you assign, if any
- An optional screenshot, if you choose to attach one
- Your settings (Notion connection details, AI provider key, voice preference) — stored locally only

**None of this data is sent to any server operated by the developer.** There is no backend server for this extension; it does not collect, log, or have access to any of your data.

## Where your data goes if you turn on optional features

These features are off by default and only activate if you configure them yourself:

- **Notion sync**: if you provide your own Notion integration key and database, your highlights (text, notes, screenshots) are sent directly from your browser to Notion's API, into your own Notion workspace. The developer never sees this data.
- **AI Knowledge Graph**: if you provide your own Anthropic or OpenAI API key, your highlighted text is sent directly from your browser to that provider's API to generate the knowledge-graph categorization. The developer never sees this data.

In both cases, the extension talks directly to the third-party service using credentials you provide — the developer has no server in the middle and cannot access this data.

## What the extension does not do

- It does not sell or transfer your data to any third party for advertising or any other purpose
- It does not use your data to determine creditworthiness or for lending purposes
- It does not use your data for any purpose other than providing the highlighting/note/sync features described above

## Data deletion

Since all data is stored locally in your browser, you can delete it at any time by removing entries from the extension's list view, or by removing the extension entirely (which clears its local storage).
